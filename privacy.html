Privacy Policy

ReadyPlayer — readyplayer.network
Effective date: November 16, 2026

In short: The ReadyPlayer website does not use signup forms or website accounts. The membership community runs on Beagle, where identity is based on a key you control. The ReadyPlayer Muse connector is read-only and does not collect personal information, process sign-ins, or handle payments.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how ReadyPlayer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with readyplayer.network, its community and services information, and its related connector integrations.

ReadyPlayer is a bilingual community and services offer for solo founders and vibe coders. Its membership community runs on Beagle using key-based identity rather than conventional signup forms. Membership tiers are Free, Basic at $9 per month, and Pro at $99 per month, paid in USDC on Solana through Beagle, with no auto-renew. ReadyPlayer also describes fractional expert roles, including CTO and Scientist roles at $2,000 per month and Engineer, Author, and Editor roles at $1,000 per month.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide to us

  • Contact and inquiry information. If you email us or ask about membership or services, we receive your email address, the name or handle you use, and the contents of your message.
  • Membership and service information. If you join or ask to join, we may process the Beagle identity, address, display name, or contact details you choose to share, the tier or service you request, and information needed to provide the membership or service.
  • Community information. If you participate in the Beagle community, we may see messages, files, meeting participation, and other information you choose to share in groups or directly with a membership manager or service provider.
  • Event or matching information. If you choose to take part in events or founder matching, we process the information you provide for that purpose, such as your interests, background, availability, and any profile information you ask us to use.

Payment-verification information

Membership payments are made in USDC on the Solana network through Beagle. Joining and payment happen inside Beagle and outside the ReadyPlayer Muse connector. To verify a payment, you may provide a transaction signature or related confirmation details. Wallet addresses and blockchain transactions may also be publicly visible on the Solana network.

We do not collect or store your Beagle private key, wallet seed phrase, or wallet password. Never share those credentials with us.

Card payments through Stripe are announced as coming. When card payments are available, they will be processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy; we will receive payment confirmation and limited payment details, not your full card number.

Technical and log information

Our servers and infrastructure providers may automatically record limited technical information when you visit the website. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, referring pages, timestamps, and error or security logs. We use this information to operate, secure, debug, and improve the website.

Connector information

The ReadyPlayer Muse connector is read-only. It retrieves public information from readyplayer.network and does not require an account. It does not collect personal information from Muse users, process ReadyPlayer sign-ins, or handle membership payments.

3. Cookies and analytics

We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site behavioral tracking. Our hosting, security, or infrastructure providers may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to deliver the website and protect it from abuse.

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate use of the website, such as which public pages are visited most often. If analytics are used, they are intended to provide aggregate insights rather than advertising profiles.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate and secure the website;
  • respond to inquiries and provide requested information;
  • administer memberships, verify payments, and provide community access;
  • arrange and provide fractional expert or other services you request;
  • organize events or matching when you choose to participate;
  • prevent spam, abuse, fraud, or security incidents;
  • understand aggregate website usage and improve our public information; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

5. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers. With hosting, content-delivery, security, infrastructure, communications, or analytics providers that help us operate, and only as needed for those services.
  • Community and service delivery. With membership managers, fractional experts, event collaborators, or other members where sharing is reasonably necessary to provide a service you requested or where you choose to share information in a group setting.
  • Public blockchain records. Solana transactions, wallet addresses, memos, and transaction signatures may be visible to anyone through public blockchain explorers. We cannot make a public blockchain record private.
  • Legal and safety. If we believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, or is reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or ReadyPlayer.
  • Business changes. In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of the service, subject to applicable law.

6. Beagle and other third-party services

The membership community runs on Beagle. Identity there is based on a cryptographic key generated and controlled by the user rather than a ReadyPlayer website account. Beagle, Solana wallets and networks, and other third-party services have their own terms and privacy practices. We are not responsible for how those independent services process information.

Information you post or send in community groups or meetings may be visible to other participants. Do not share private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or sensitive personal or business information in community spaces.

If you access ReadyPlayer information through a third-party AI platform, that platform may process your prompts and interactions under its own privacy policy. The ReadyPlayer connector itself is read-only and does not receive your Beagle credentials or payment information from that platform.

7. Data retention

We retain technical logs only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, operations, debugging, and legal compliance. Inquiry and service records may be retained so we can respond, administer memberships or services, verify payments, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Community content may remain available while it is part of the relevant group or service, subject to the features and policies of Beagle and any removal we can reasonably perform.

Public blockchain records are maintained by the Solana network and cannot be deleted by ReadyPlayer.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect the information we process. No website, transmission, blockchain transaction, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protecting your Beagle identity and wallet depends in part on keeping your private keys and recovery material secure and under your control.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request or ask a privacy question, email us at the address below. We may need to verify your request before responding. Some information may need to be retained for security, payment-verification, legal, accounting, or operational reasons, and public blockchain information cannot be deleted by us.

10. Children

ReadyPlayer is intended for founders and business users and is not directed to children under the age at which they can consent to the processing of personal information under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us.
