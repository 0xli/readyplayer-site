CTO 这一档不是按小时卖咨询,也不是给你半个 CTO。它是一个联席 CTO:agent 保证 100%——它常驻、不下班、记得住你所有的技术上下文;真人再叠 10%–100%——判断力、方向、架构把关、技术选型、招人、以及什么不该做。他不替你写代码:CTO 的价值在决定怎么做,不在动手做;活由 agent 和你的人干。加起来是 110%–200% 的 CTO 投入,比全职还多。预算到位,可以派一个 CTO 专门负责你的专项。
最接近的说法是高级 FDE:工程师进到你的现场,按你的实际情况做东西,而不是把一套通用方案推给你。大多数早期公司请不起全职 CTO,又恰恰是最需要的时候——这一档就是为这个缺口开的。

没有年费锁定,没有销售电话。想走随时走——你的身份和你的联系人本来就在你自己手里。

The CTO tier is not consulting by the hour, and it is not half a CTO. It is a joint CTO: the agent holds 100% — always on, never off the clock, never losing your context; the person adds 10%–100% — judgement, direction, architecture, tech choices, hiring, and what not to build. He does not write your code. A CTO earns their keep deciding how the work gets done, not doing it; the agent and your people build. Together that is 110%–200% of a CTO — more than full-time, not less. Fund it properly and one is assigned to your project outright.
The closest name for it is a senior FDE: an engineer who works inside your situation and builds for it, instead of handing you a generic answer. Most early companies cannot afford a full-time CTO at exactly the point they most need one — that is the gap this tier exists for.

No annual lock-in, no sales call. Leave whenever — your identity and your contacts were always yours to take.
